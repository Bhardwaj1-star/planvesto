# Funding Optimisation Capabilities

## Purpose

Identify how an investor's existing financial resources can be managed more effectively to fund their goals. These capabilities must work with resources already present in the investor's financial position; they must not assume that the investor will bring in new money.

## 1. Cash-Flow Optimisation — Budgeting

Cash-Flow Optimisation belongs to the **Budgeting** capability.

It should analyse the investor's existing income, expenses, recurring commitments, savings and current contributions to identify whether sustainable monthly cash flow can be freed up or improved for goal funding.

Expected outputs:
- A transparent view of current monthly cash flow and the verified surplus.
- Potential expense or commitment adjustments, with their monthly cash-flow impact.
- The resulting sustainable additional monthly capacity, if any.
- The assumptions, trade-offs and evidence behind each proposed change.

Constraints:
- Do not assume additional income or external/new money.
- Do not treat a proposed expense reduction as achieved until the investor confirms or implements it.
- Do not silently change existing commitments or contributions.
- Distinguish current verified surplus from hypothetical surplus under a proposed budget.

## 2. Asset Optimisation — Strategy Builder

Asset Optimisation belongs to the **Strategy Builder** capability.

It should analyse the investor's existing assets and portfolio to identify suitable ways those resources could be managed, reallocated or made available for goal funding.

Potential methods may include:
- Reallocation of existing investments.
- Redemption or partial withdrawal, where appropriate.
- Use of asset maturity proceeds when timing is evidenced.
- Restructuring existing holdings or allocations, where suitable.

Expected outputs:
- Identified assets and the amount potentially available for goal funding.
- Proposed asset-management method and timing.
- Liquidity, risk, tax and other material implications, where data is available.
- Evidence, assumptions, restrictions and any unresolved information.

Constraints:
- Do not assume ownership, availability, liquidity or redemption value without supporting data.
- Do not treat a proposed redemption or reallocation as completed.
- Do not recommend an action without considering its impact on the investor's broader portfolio and other goals.
- Do not assume that new assets or external funds will be introduced.

## Capability Boundary

- **Budgeting** identifies potential monthly funding capacity from existing cash flow.
- **Strategy Builder** identifies potential goal funding from existing assets.
- These are separate capabilities with separate responsibilities; neither is a sub-module of Goal Planner.
- Their verified outputs may be consumed by goal-feasibility and planning workflows as funding inputs. Proposed or hypothetical outputs must remain clearly labelled as such.

## Core Principle

Funding plans must be grounded in the investor's existing, evidenced resources. Step-Up SIP capacity or lump-sum availability must not be invented or assumed: monthly capacity must be supported by Cash-Flow Optimisation, and any lump-sum or asset-based funding must be supported by Asset Optimisation.
